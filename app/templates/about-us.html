About Nyctograph

What is Nyctograph?

Nyctograph is a dream recording and research platform designed to help users document, analyze, and understand their dreams.

Whether you're curious about your own dreams, want to explore patterns in your subconscious, or wish to contribute data to dream science research, Nyctograph provides a secure and convenient platform for you.

Platform Features

Nyctograph offers the following core features:

  • Dream Recording: Document your dreams in detail, including descriptions, sensory experiences, sleep duration, and more
  • Follow-up Reflections: Return to your dream entries later to add new thoughts, insights, or interpretations as your understanding evolves
  • Tagging System: Use rich tags to categorize your dreams, including content, types (like lucid dreams, nightmares), and real-life context
  • Personal Statistics: View your dream patterns and trends to understand your sleep and dream cycles
  • Global Statistics: Explore anonymized dream data from all users to discover common dream phenomena and patterns
  • Privacy Controls: Maintain complete control over your data, choosing whether to make dreams public or keep them private

How It Works

Regular User Accounts:

  • After registering, you can begin recording your dreams
  • Each dream entry can include a title, detailed description, sensory experiences, sleep duration, geographic location, and more
  • Add multiple tags to each dream for easy searching and analysis
  • Return to any dream entry later to add follow-up reflections as new insights emerge
  • View all your records and personal statistics on the "My Dreams" page
  • In account settings, choose whether to allow your dream data to be anonymized for global statistics and research

Research Institution Accounts:

  • Research institutions can register dedicated accounts to access and analyze anonymized dream data
  • Research accounts cannot record dreams but can use advanced filtering to view qualifying data
  • All data provided to research institutions is strictly anonymized

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Username, email, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information (optional): Birth date, gender, medical conditions, geographic location
  • Dream Data: All dream content, tags, sensory experiences, etc. that you record

Data Usage

How your data is used depends on your privacy settings:

  • Private Dreams: Only visible to you, not used for any statistics or research
  • Public Dreams (must be enabled in account settings):
    • Included in global statistics after anonymization
    • Accessible to research institutions after removing all personally identifiable information
    • Used for academic research and the advancement of dream science

Data Security

Your data belongs to you, and we are committed to protecting it:

  • We will never share, sell, or misuse your personal information
  • Your data is stored securely and protected from unauthorized access
  • You maintain complete control over your data at all times
  • You can modify your privacy settings whenever you want
  • You can choose whether each dream entry is public or private
  • If you disable public data sharing, your dreams are immediately removed from global statistics and research data
  • You can edit or delete your dream entries at any time

Research and Data Usage

Research Purpose

Public dream data will be used to advance dream science research, including but not limited to:

  • Research on correlations between dream content and real-life events
  • Analysis of dream patterns and characteristics across different populations
  • Distribution and patterns of sensory experiences in dreams
  • Research on conditions affecting special dream types (such as lucid dreams and nightmares)

Anonymization Process

All data provided to research institutions undergoes strict anonymization:

  • Removal of all personally identifiable information (usernames, emails, etc.)
  • Geographic location information generalized to city level
  • Birth dates converted to age ranges
  • Dream content does not include any identifiers linking to specific users

Data Access Rules

Research institutions accessing data must follow these rules:

  • Can only access dream data that users have explicitly allowed to be public
  • Must not attempt to reverse-identify or link specific users
  • Data may only be used for academic research and scientific analysis, not commercial purposes
  • Must comply with relevant data protection regulations and ethical guidelines
